You come home to Agartala, to your grandfather's bari on the bank of the Haora, behind Maharaja Bir Bikram Stadium. Shibnagar, Math Chowmuhani, Ramnagar and Joynagar are all around you, every road, lane and pond where it really is. Grow paddy and Queen pineapple, tap rubber at dawn, ride tomtoms round the paras, help the neighbours, and keep the festivals from Garia Puja to Durga Puja at Modern Club.

Type a neighbourhood or chowmuhani, or pick a spot on the map. Your house and Grandpa will be there; the family fields stay by the Haora.

How to play

WASD
Walk. Hold Shift to run, or press R to switch running on and off. Drag the mouse to turn the camera, scroll to zoom, press C to reset the camera.
E
Do the thing in front of you: till, sow, water, harvest, tap, cut, talk, trade, take a tomtom. The prompt at the bottom says what E will do.
Z
Sleep till 06:00. Only after 19:00 or before 05:00.
M
Map of the city.
L
Your farm: farm code and sign-in, neighbours and phrasebook, Grandpa's album, festivals, and the boards.
E near a vehicle
Get on. Then W/S to speed up, brake and reverse, A/D to steer, Space to brake, E to get off. You start with Grandpa's cycle-rickshaw; Gopal sells a bicycle, scooter, motorbike, tomtom and car. Stop a rickshaw or tomtom at any tomtom stand to pick up a passenger for a fare. Every tomtom stand also has a rental point: rent by the hour and return it at any of them.
P
Photo mode: hide the HUD, frame a shot and save a postcard.

The city is real: streets, ponds and buildings come from OpenStreetMap. Tomtom stands (the green e-rickshaws) take you across town for ₹20. Paddy grows in the low lunga, pineapple on the tilla. Crops grow only on days they are watered; rain waters everything. Rubber gives latex once a day between 05:00 and 11:00, and 3 cups roll into one sheet. Work uses energy: eat at the food stalls by the tomtom stands, rest, or sleep. The game saves on its own.

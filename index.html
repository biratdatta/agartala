You come home to Agartala, to your grandfather's house in whichever para you call home, and the family fields at Bhati Abhoynagar. Every road, lane and pond of the city centre is where it really is, and its 30,000 houses follow the real streets. Grow paddy and Queen pineapple, tap rubber at dawn, ride tomtoms through the real streets, help the neighbours, and keep the festivals from Garia Puja to Durga Puja at Ujjayanta Palace.

Type a neighbourhood or chowmuhani, or pick a spot on the map. Your house and Grandpa will be there; the family fields stay at Bhati Abhoynagar.

How to play

WASD
Walk. Hold Shift to run, or press R to switch running on and off. Drag the mouse to turn the camera, scroll to zoom, press C to reset the camera.
E
Do the thing in front of you: till, sow, water, harvest, tap, cut, talk, trade, take a tomtom. The prompt at the bottom says what E will do.
Z
Sleep till 06:00. Only after 19:00 or before 05:00.
M
Map of the city.
L
Your farm: leaderboard, farm name, farm code and sign-in.

The city is real: streets, ponds and buildings come from OpenStreetMap. Tomtom stands (the green e-rickshaws) take you across town for ₹20. Paddy grows in the low lunga, pineapple on the tilla. Crops grow only on days they are watered; rain waters everything. Rubber gives latex once a day between 05:00 and 11:00, and 3 cups roll into one sheet. The game saves on its own in this browser.
